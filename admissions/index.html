COMMON QUESTIONS

Here for all
your questions.

Is the learning path result a formal school placement?+

No. It is a sample content recommendation only. Admissions staff would determine actual program availability, age requirements, and suitability.

What happens after I submit the school visit request?+

In this presentation prototype, you will see an on-screen confirmation. No message is sent to the school. For production, a real admissions contact or CRM would be connected.

Are application dates and tuition available?+

Not yet. Verified tuition, intake dates, requirements, and other admissions documents would be supplied by Krishna prior to production.

Can I speak with the school instead?+

Yes. A contact page demonstrates how enquiries could be directed to the appropriate school team. Official contact information remains a placeholder in this concept.
